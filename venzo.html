A little about us

We're changing the way
money moves.

Venzo is building a simpler way for you to move through your day - with less hassles at checkout, and more control over your money. We believe financial services should work around people's everyday lives, not make them more complicated. This is why we exist.

01 / OUR MANDATE

Simplify the everyday.

To simplify everyday financial interactions by making payments, money transfers, and personal money management more accessible, seamless, and convenient.

02 / OUR VISION

A more effortless financial future.

To become the preferred payment and money management platform for a generation that expects more from its money, creating a future where financial interactions are effortless and connected.

03 / OUR MISSION

Make money easier to move.

To eliminate the everyday friction associated with making payments and managing money through simple, intuitive, and accessible financial technology.
